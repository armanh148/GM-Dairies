# GM Dairies Karachi — Online Dairy Store & Subscription Web App 🥛🧈

A responsive, high-converting React.js web application engineered specifically for **GM Dairies (Karachi)**.

---

### 🌟 Business Background & Pitch Objective
* **Business:** GM Dairies
* **Depot & Store:** Near Moti Mahal, Block 2, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi
* **Hotline:** +92 310 3510076 (Active Call & WhatsApp)
* **Rating:** 4.8 ★ (41+ Customer Reviews)
* **Previous Presence:** Instagram & Facebook only
* **Core Pain Point Solved:** Customers repeatedly comment on Instagram asking *"Online available?"* — This platform turns that social media engagement into recurring daily milk subscriptions and direct WhatsApp orders.

---

### ✨ Features Built

1. **"You Asked: Online Available?" Instagram Story Section**
   * Features actual simulated Instagram customer queries asking about Gulshan & Johar deliveries.
   * Highlights GM Dairies' official verification reply and instant WhatsApp ordering.

2. **Interactive Morning Milk Subscription Planner**
   * Select Milk Variety: 100% Pure Buffalo Milk (Full Cream 6.8% fat) vs Farm Fresh Cow Milk (4.2% fat).
   * Daily Quantity selector: 1L, 2L, 3L, 4L.
   * Delivery Frequency: Everyday (7 days/week), Alternate Days, or Weekdays Only.
   * Morning Delivery Slots: 5:30 AM – 6:30 AM, 6:30 AM – 7:30 AM, 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM.
   * Dynamic monthly savings & bill calculation with 5% subscriber discount.
   * 1-Click WhatsApp Subscription activation pre-filled with customer address & schedule.

3. **Product Catalog & E-Commerce Store**
   * GM 100% Pure Buffalo Milk (Pouch & Chilled Bottles) — Rs. 270/L
   * GM Farm Fresh Cow Milk — Rs. 280/L
   * GM Shudh Desi Ghee (Bilona Churned, Danedar) — 500g (Rs. 1,550) & 1KG (Rs. 2,950)
   * GM Fresh White Butter (Desi Makhan) — Rs. 850 / 500g
   * GM Thick Sweet / Saada Dahi (Traditional Curd) — Rs. 340 / 1KG
   * GM Pure Malai Paneer & Khoya — Rs. 680 / 500g

4. **Karachi Morning Delivery Coverage Checker**
   * Real-time area filter for Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Gulistan-e-Johar, PECHS, Clifton, DHA, North Nazimabad, Malir Cantt, etc.
   * Shows drop timing, minimum order requirements, and free delivery thresholds.

5. **5-Point Purity & Hygiene Standard**
   * 0% Added Water (Lactometer tested daily)
   * 100% Hormone & Oxytocin-free cattle
   * Chilled below 4°C within 15 minutes of milking
   * Own-packaged sealed tamper-proof containers

6. **Interactive Cart Drawer & WhatsApp Checkout Engine**
   * Live quantity adjust, subtotal, and Karachi delivery fee calculation.
   * Captures customer name, phone, Karachi delivery address, preferred slot, and special notes.
   * Generates a WhatsApp formatted order message sent directly to `+92 310 3510076`.

7. **Flagship Location & Walk-In Information**
   * Near Moti Mahal, Block 2, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi.
   * Timings: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM (7 days a week).
   * Google Maps directions button.
